# How to Print Your CV to PDF

This guide will help you print your CV to PDF with optimal formatting and without browser headers/footers.

## Quick Steps

1. Open your CV in the application
2. Click on the **Preview** tab
3. Click the **Print CV** button (or press `Ctrl+P` / `Cmd+P`)
4. In the print dialog, configure these settings:

### Print Dialog Settings

**Important Settings:**
- **Destination**: Save as PDF
- **Pages**: All
- **Layout**: Portrait
- **Paper Size**: A4 (or Letter in US)
- **Margins**: None or Minimal
- **Scale**: 100% (Default)

### Remove Browser Headers and Footers

**Chrome/Edge:**
1. In the print dialog, click **More settings**
2. **Uncheck** "Headers and footers"
3. Click **Save** or **Print**

**Firefox:**
1. In the print dialog, click **More settings** or the gear icon
2. Under **Headers & Footers**, set all dropdowns to **--blank--**
3. Click **Print**

**Safari:**
1. In the print dialog, uncheck **Print headers and footers**
2. Click **PDF** → **Save as PDF**

## Tips for Best Results

1. **Use the Preview tab** - Always print from the Preview tab, not the Edit tab
2. **Check the preview** - Before saving, review the PDF preview to ensure formatting looks correct
3. **Adjust margins** - If content is cut off, try adjusting the margin settings
4. **One-page CV** - For shorter CVs, you can fit everything on one page by:
   - Using concise bullet points
   - Removing extra white space
   - Prioritizing most important information

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Date/time appears at top of PDF**
- Solution: Make sure "Headers and footers" is unchecked in print settings

**Problem: CV is split across too many pages**
- Solution: The app is optimized to fit content efficiently. If still too long:
  - Reduce font sizes in the browser (Ctrl/Cmd + `-`)
  - Edit content to be more concise
  - Check margins are set to "None" or "Minimal"

**Problem: Colors don't print correctly**
- Solution: In print dialog, enable "Background graphics" (Chrome/Edge)

**Problem: Content is cut off**
- Solution: Set margins to "Default" or "Minimal" instead of "None"

## Professional Printing

For the best professional results:
- Use **Portrait** orientation
- Select **A4** or **Letter** size
- Ensure **Headers and Footers** are disabled
- Print at **100% scale** (no shrinking)
- Save as PDF for digital distribution
